# German pedestrian satire dictionary

## Purpose

This is the durable writing and visual brief for personalized ordinary pedestrians. It is a selective comedy taxonomy, not a claim to describe every German person or a demographic model of Germany. The dominant street read is the grumpy German middle-class proceduralist: irritated before anything has happened, equipped with a small rule or household system, and absolutely certain the public realm is being used incorrectly. The crowd remains contemporary, urban and multicultural because the recurring joke is how many different people can become equally committed to a tiny procedure.

The appearance is not the archetype. Names, skin tones, family histories, ages and body types may rotate independently from the comic behavior. Never make ethnicity, nationality, accent, religion, disability, gender identity, refugee status or poverty the defect being mocked. Aim the joke at status performance, consumer ritual, rule policing, administrative certainty and the conversion of private preferences into public procedure.

`Potato` is permitted here only as a literal anthropomorphic vegetable: a friendly potato with legs, socks, sandals and a towel. Do not use it as a label for a human ethnicity or migration background.

The production order is deliberately weighted rather than demographically neutral. Quiet-hours, DIN, towel-reservation, waste-sorting and potato officials occupy fourteen of every sixteen placements; cargo logistics and bio-vegan lifestyle each occupy one. This keeps the immediate tone grumpy, rule-policing and middle-class, with vegan/hippy material as a rare accent rather than the thesis of the crowd. Personal names and appearance still rotate independently, so “middle class” describes the performed behavior and props, never an ethnicity.

All eight walk horizontal and vertical sidewalk routes and share one animation contract: eight authored contact/down/passing/up keys for both legs in side, front/down and back/up rows; three motion-compensated in-betweens per interval and loop seam; 32 runtime frames in each direction; a registered center and baseline; and horizontal mirroring only for left travel. Runtime speech is circular-proximity based and repeats from the archetype's no-repeat regional pool. Berlin uses Denglisch; Deutschland uses German only.

## Expansion checklist

Before adding another pedestrian type:

1. Name the behavior or institution being exaggerated in one sentence. If the answer is a protected identity, stop.
2. Give the character one legible prop and one legible silhouette; do not pile on national costumes.
3. Write paired Berlin-Denglisch and German-only barks that begin with an alleged rule, process or jurisdiction problem.
4. Rotate personal names independently from the archetype and maintain a visibly mixed crowd across the complete set.
5. Author or generate the complete eight-key gait before interpolation. Passing frames must show the legs crossing, while head, torso, scale and foot baseline remain registered.
6. Run the checksum-bound critical animation gate and real-game desktop/mobile playtest before shipping. A changed sprite is unapproved until every anatomical and loop item is visually signed again.
