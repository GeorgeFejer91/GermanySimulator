## Detection and fidelity boundary

The browser supplies OS and mobile hints; Chromium may also return a model string through [`getHighEntropyValues(["model"])`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/NavigatorUAData/getHighEntropyValues), subject to browser support and policy. [User-agent reduction](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Guides/User-agent_reduction) removes model details from many ordinary UA strings. The game uses a Samsung model or Samsung Internet hint only to choose the Samsung family. Otherwise Android uses Google Phone, iOS uses the iPhone full-screen family, and unknown browsers use neutral tap controls. `?callUi=ios|google|samsung|generic` is a review override.

Web pages cannot read the installed default phone app, its call gesture setting, wallpaper/theme, carrier customization, lock state, call-screening choice, or native incoming-call display setting. The references establish faithful *family and state* layouts; no automatic selector can guarantee pixel-identical native chrome on every physical phone. The staged call retains the game's German police conversation. Phone audio uses maximum browser volume automatically, without a visible volume control.

The comparison's stills and manufacturer instructions were directly inspected through their source pages. YouTube playback was throttled in the research browser, so the linked videos are additional review references rather than frame-by-frame measurements. CSS geometry is validated against rendered browser screenshots and overflow checks, not inferred from video metadata.

## German police caller display

Checked 2026-10-08. The [Bundesnetzagentur's caller-number guidance](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Vportal/TK/Aerger/Faelle/Manipulation/start.html) says that `110` is never a lawful incoming caller number, and police emergency-call centres use the local numbers of their stations when calling back. [Police prevention guidance](https://www.polizei-beratung.de/fileadmin/Medien/285-IB-Vorsicht-Falscher-Polizist-am-Telefon.pdf) also rules out genuine outgoing calls identified as `110`. These sources do not establish that every Kriminalpolizei office withholds its number, nor a universal police-specific caller name or screen.

For this episode, the incoming caller remains unidentified until the officer speaks after answering. Display `Unbekannte Nummer` in German and `Unknown number` in English on the existing iPhone, Google, Samsung and generic layouts. There is no invented police badge, saved contact, caller number or `110`. This is the scene's unavailable-number treatment, not a claim about all German police calls or an exact native private-number string on every dialer.

The browser's [client hints](https://wicg.github.io/ua-client-hints/) can expose the OS and sometimes an Android model, but do not supply the selected dialer, its theme or its incoming-call settings. Model data may be empty or withheld. The current automatic family selection remains an inference. Published references and silent browser emulation cover the implemented layout and gestures; native incoming calls on physical iPhones and Android handsets have not been compared in this task. This change affects phone text only, so the world physics and camera review gates do not apply.
