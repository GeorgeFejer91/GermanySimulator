# Game-object consistency verification

This protocol owns physical interaction, pedestrian routing, camera visibility, sprite depth and relative scale in the canonical root game. `game.js` owns simulation and collision; `world3d.js` renders that state; `3d.html` is the direct diagnostic entry. Reuse their existing geometry, actors, movement and material paths. A new physics engine, navigation service or second world is unnecessary. The user requested these independent review gates on 2026-10-08.

## Required reviewers and acceptance

For every candidate that changes world objects, placement, movement, collision, pedestrian behavior, animation, camera effects, sprite layering, model fitting or scale, the integrator assigns **two independent read-only subagents**:

- **Physics consistency reviewer:** only solid-object separation, body-clear pedestrian routes/crossings, passing/yielding, corner recovery, displacement-driven animation and bounded road-based police-car driving (P6) and complete-body off-map car lifecycle (P7). No source writes or approval of their own implementation.
- **Camera and scale consistency reviewer:** only actual obstruction, camera/material fade, 2D/3D depth ordering, model/fallback bounds and people/car/door/building/landmark proportions. No source writes or approval of their own implementation.

Both review the same integrated candidate, identified by base commit plus SHA-256 of every affected source/test/asset (or a clean commit). Freeze that candidate while reviews run. They may consume evidence from the author but label it supplied; their fresh checks and findings remain separate. After repairs, rerun the affected matrix rows and return both reviewers to the new candidate. The author cannot be the sole approver. If agent tools are unavailable, record the unrun review and keep acceptance outstanding; do not silently replace independent review with self-review.

Only a task unrelated to world behavior (for example isolated documentation or dialogue spelling) may omit these roles, with an explicit scope reason in its report. A skipped or unexecuted check is `NOT RUN`, never `PASS`. The optional ChatDev graph includes separate read-only `Physics` and `Camera` agents before `Review` and `QA`; a focused production stage still needs these two reviews before world-change acceptance. ChatDev has no browser/image/device tool, so its source review cannot establish rendered correctness.

Use the [shared review report](./chatdev/README.md#shared-contracts-and-convergence): candidate; reviewer and role; `PASS`/`FAIL`/`NOT RUN` **per matrix row and evidence type**; actual commands/scenarios; measured values and evidence paths; supplied vs fresh evidence; limits; findings and repair owner. Overall acceptance stays outstanding while a required check is `FAIL` or `NOT RUN`. Static graph validation establishes available roles and tool scopes, not that any review ran.

Before dispatch, the integrator selects affected rows and records a reason for any out-of-scope row. Both specialist roles remain mandatory for a world-affecting candidate. Collision/routing changes require P1–P4, gait changes P4–P5, vehicle-driving changes also require P6–P7, visibility/material/depth changes C1–C4, and scale/placement changes P1–P2 plus C1–C5. X1 covers the affected browser scenarios. A visibility change always includes proximity-negative, loaded/fallback and restoration cases; a scale change always includes the human/car/door/landmark comparison. Broader regression coverage is warranted by a new failure, not automatically by every spelling change.

## Ground and rendering contracts

- Ground solids have a collision footprint that covers their blocking visible body. For furniture, use declared fitted ground dimensions and the procedural fallback footprint; height is not a substitute for depth. Explicit fixture radii and typed norm-object radii must be shared by actor, vehicle, traffic and train contacts. Decorations and airborne actors may differ only through an explicit existing gameplay rule. A faded wall remains physically solid. Trains and vehicles need their complete oriented body and swept path, rather than only their center point. Spawning, following, escorting, ordinary walking and collision recovery obey the same separation rules.
- Ordinary citizens stay on the union of sidewalks, pedestrian walkways and marked crossings, including their body radius and clearance. Sampling only the center is insufficient. Route changes, quiz approaches, side steps, spawn points and recovery must preserve this rule. The player's intentional jaywalking and authored police pursuit remain gameplay actions; do not use them as permission for ordinary crowd routes to cut across roads or grass.
- People cannot pass through each other, the player or ground solids. Opposing walkers use available lateral space; if there is insufficient width, yield/wait or choose another legal route. A crowded legal passage must not be solved by disabling collision, teleporting, trespassing or making walking frames imply progress while feet stay planted. Turning and planted reaction frames are distinct from walking progress.
- Police cars drive with bounded signed speed, acceleration, braking, steering angle/rate and a wheelbase-based curved path. They cannot spin while stationary, strafe like pedestrians, instantly reverse velocity or turn their body through people/walls. Sweep the complete oriented body through translation and rotation. Use road-clear interception targets; off-road pursuit belongs to foot officers. When blocked, brake and use a bounded collision-safe reversing/turning maneuver or wait. Sirens and pursuit never excuse solid-body overlap or teleportation.
- Cars enter from beyond the full map boundary and drive out before removal or lane recycling. Live appearance/removal requires the complete car envelope outside both WORLD and the actual camera view, including loaded/fallback geometry, steering and platform elevation. Initial level population may be preplaced. Clearing police response marks cars for physical departure, preserving collision until they leave. Only the explicit vortex swallowing effect may remove a car on-map; its replacement still begins outside the map and camera.
- Fade a building **only when its currently visible opaque geometry intersects the segment from the actual camera to the player's visible body**. Footprint overlap, contact, distance, a screen-space bounding rectangle or a ground-only ray cannot alone establish obstruction. Check actual loaded meshes and procedural fallback meshes, their height, rotation, visibility and current world transforms. Authored transparent glass is not an opaque blocker. A building beside/behind the player, a low roof below every player-body sightline and a gap/archway with a clear sightline stay unchanged.
- Fade and restoration interpolate by elapsed time with bounded delay and no edge flicker. Only the obstructing building instances fade, including multiple simultaneous blockers. Clone per-instance materials, multiply their authored opacity by the fade, and restore authored opacity, transparency and depth-writing state. Native glass must retain its authored transparency throughout.
- Sprites stay camera-facing with stable foot pivots. Opaque parts obey real depth, and transparent atlas pixels do not hide later geometry. A person in front of a wall is visible, a person behind an opaque wall is hidden, and the player is readable through an intentionally faded blocker. Avoid global always-on-top character layers and sorting that puts a person in front of every building.
- Compare rendered world-space extents after fitting, including procedural fallbacks; source GLB bounds or parcel area alone do not prove scale. Human doors clear the displayed person, cars read as cars beside people, and a major government landmark is visibly much larger than ordinary shops/office parcels. Preserve authored landmark proportions through uniform scale and align displayed bases with their collision parcel. Perspective must not be used to conceal a miniature Bundestag. Any intentional caricature/monument exaggeration belongs in its existing owning product document and still gets a relational scale review.

## Executable verification matrix

Record initial position, route/heading, actor radii/solid dimensions, elapsed simulated time, frame step, camera transform and loaded/fallback state for each scenario. Use existing deterministic Node harnesses against production functions, then reproduce representative cases in the browser. A source-regex assertion is source evidence only; a CPU geometry check does not establish rendered depth, animation, visual fade or device performance. A screenshot proves one frame; record a timed sequence for collision recovery and smooth fade. For articulated or pitched cars, verify transformed mesh vertices or precise post-transform bounds at attainable steering/ramp extrema; upright dimensions alone can miss a projecting bonnet, while transforming already-conservative mesh bounding corners can overestimate it.

Use frame steps of 1/60 s and 1/30 s plus a 0.1 s delayed frame. Check every accepted movement substep/swept segment, not only endpoints. For ordinary grounded circles use minimum center distance `rA + rB` with at most 0.01 simulation-unit numerical tolerance; for static bodies use their production inflated footprint and the same tolerance. If the runtime uses another justified contact epsilon, record it and prove it does not permit a visibly overlapping body. The deadlines below are acceptance limits, not new runtime configuration.

The current kinematic driver bounds propulsion and controlled braking; rejecting an unsafe swept step can immediately set speed to zero. Record these contact/road-boundary stops separately from controlled braking, with raw signed-speed changes and body legality. They do not prove realistic collision impulses or bounded braking during contact, and cannot excuse over-rate propulsion or a direct forward/reverse velocity flip.

Browser sessions follow [silent background testing](./AGENT-START.md#silent-background-browser-tests): isolate and mute media, Web Audio and speech before navigation, keep focus unchanged, and do not mute the user's PC. Keep audio state active for timing tests. Physical-device, perceptual listening and browser evidence are separate; report unavailable coverage as `NOT RUN`. No external paid ChatDev/model execution, credential access or publishing is implied by this protocol.
