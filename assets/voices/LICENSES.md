# Pedestrian voice clips

The three `thorsten-angry-*.mp3` files are trimmed shipping copies of German
"angry | wütend" recordings from **Thorsten-Voice Dataset 2021.06 emotional**
version 2.0, recorded by Thorsten Müller and optimized by Dominik Kreutz.

- Source: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5525023
- Dataset mirror/API: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Thorsten-Voice/TV-44kHz-Full
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Conversion: original 44.1 kHz mono WAV from the `TV-2021.06-Emotional`
  subset, encoded locally as mono MP3 at 64 kbit/s and 22.05 kHz.
