# Kenney Train Kit subset

The three local GLB files in this directory come from **Train Kit 1.1** by
Kenney. The pack is dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons
Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0).

- Creator: Kenney — https://kenney.nl/
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/train-kit
- Download mirror used: https://opengameart.org/content/train-kit
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Pack creation date: 2024-09-22
- Additional pack credits: Guus Vermeulen and Tony Schaer

The game uses the models unchanged and repeats them to form the fictional
red-and-white `AMT-BAHN` perimeter trains. No Deutsche Bahn or Märklin logo,
word mark, recording, or branded texture is included.
