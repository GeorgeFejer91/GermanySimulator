The source STL was converted to GLB, changed from Z-up to glTF's Y-up,
normalized to a 2.35-metre vehicle length, centered on its lateral and
longitudinal axes, grounded at Y=0, and assigned a neutral material. The game
clones and recolors that material for Berlin traffic. The converted GLB is a
modified version distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. No
Volkswagen logo, texture, or endorsement is included.

The wheels, hubs, glazing, bumpers, and lamps surrounding the mesh are original
runtime geometry authored for Germany Simulator and are not part of the source
STL or the converted GLB.

The downloaded STL is not shipped as a duplicate runtime/source asset. Its
hashes above preserve the conversion input record.
