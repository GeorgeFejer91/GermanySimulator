## Kenney City Kit (Industrial) 2.0

Creator: Kenney
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial
Downloaded: 2026-09-19
Source package: `kenney_city-kit-industrial_2.0.zip`

Selected files were renamed for their in-game role:

- `building-l.glb` → `coal-building.glb`
- `chimney-basic.glb` → `coal-stack.glb`
- `chimney-large.glb` → `cooling-tower.glb`

Source: https://3dassets.dev/packs/nuclear-power-station-and-control-room
