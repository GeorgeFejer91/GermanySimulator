Sources & accuracy

This architectural study combines Berlin's official cadastral and LoD2 exterior dimensions, roofs and annexes with reference-based façade and interior detail. Room dimensions, partitions, floor elevations and equipment positions remain provisional.

The main club, foyer, Panorama Bar, Säule, Halle and garden can be explored. Lab.oratory, service rooms, bathrooms, fine surface detail and several connecting spaces still need verified references and modelling. This is not a complete surveyed digital twin.
